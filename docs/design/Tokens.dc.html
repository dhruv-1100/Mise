Contrast, measured

Every pair below was computed from the oklch values, not eyeballed. The audit found a real problem: accent, herb and warn share lightness 0.565 so they are perceptually even — but WCAG luminance is not perceptual lightness, and herb and warn landed at 4.0:1 and 4.4:1 on ground, under the 4.5 that small text needs. The matched trio stays for fills and icons, where 3:1 applies; herb-text and warn-text carry text.
