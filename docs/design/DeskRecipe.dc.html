Aloo Paratha

Chef Ranveer Brar· 8:00 · North Indian
Method
1
Add whole wheat flour, gram flour and ghee to a bowl. Mix well and form a crumb-like mixture.
2
Add water as required and knead a soft dough. Cover and set aside for 20 minutes.
3
Add oil to the dough and knead a little until absorbed.
4
Mix the boiled potatoes with ginger, green chilli, coriander and the spices.
5
Divide the dough into equal portions and form small lemon-sized balls.
6
Roll them into a flat disc and add the prepared stuffing in the centre.
7
Roll into a potli, remove the excess dough and roll back into a disc.
8
Heat a tawa and roast on both sides for 30 seconds each, brushing with ghee.
9
Garnish with cubes of butter and serve hot with yogurt and pickle.
